Questions, corrections, and press enquiries are all welcome.

How to reach us

Use the contact form on this site. It reaches us directly, and you do not need an account to use it. We do not publish a personal email address, which keeps the inbox usable and the replies faster.

Corrections

This is the message we most want to receive. If a price, a size, a concentration, or an ingredient name is wrong, tell us:

  • which article,
  • which figure,
  • and, if you have it, where the correct figure is published.

We check it against the source, fix it in place, and add a dated correction note to the article. We do not quietly edit numbers.

For brands

We are happy to receive corrected ingredient or pricing information, and we will use it if it is verifiable on a page anyone can check. To be clear about what we cannot offer: we do not accept payment, products, or gifts in exchange for coverage, for a particular conclusion, or for the removal of an unflattering comparison. We also do not run sponsored posts.

If your product's disclosed figures have changed, that is genuinely useful to us - this category changes formulas and packaging faster than most.

What we cannot help with

We cannot give personal skincare or medical advice, diagnose a reaction, or tell you whether a product is safe for you. For anything persistent, painful, or worsening, please speak to a board-certified dermatologist or another qualified clinician.
