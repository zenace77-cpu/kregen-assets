This page describes what happens to data when you visit K-Regen Lab. It reflects how the site actually runs today, not a generic template.

Who runs this site

K-Regen Lab is an independent publication hosted on Blogger, a service operated by Google. Because the site runs on Blogger, Google processes standard technical data as part of serving the pages.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. We do not run our own analytics, we do not operate a newsletter, we do not have user accounts, and we do not ask for or store your name, email address, or payment details.

What the platform collects

  • Blogger / Google: as the host, Google may record standard server information such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and time of request, and may set cookies for its own services. This is governed by Google's own privacy policy.
  • Comments: if you leave a comment, Blogger handles it and any profile information you choose to attach to it.
  • Contact form: if you write to us through the form, we receive the message and the address you supply, and use it only to reply.

Outbound and affiliate links

Articles link to retailers and other third-party sites. Once you follow a link, that site's own privacy practices apply, and it may set its own cookies - including affiliate tracking cookies that record that you arrived from here. We have no access to what those sites collect. See our Affiliate Disclosure for the commercial side of that relationship.

Cookies

We set no cookies of our own. Cookies you encounter here come from Google as the platform operator, and from third-party sites you choose to visit. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site remains readable without them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live - including under the GDPR in Europe and the CCPA in California - you may have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to certain processing. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, most such requests need to go to Google as the platform operator. If you have contacted us through the form and want that message deleted, ask us and we will delete it.

Children

This site is written for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If how the site handles data changes - for example when analytics or an affiliate program is added - this page is updated and the date below changes with it.
