Short version: some links on this site may earn us a commission. That never decides what we recommend, and it never changes a number in our data.

The policy

K-Regen Lab may use affiliate links. When you buy through one, the retailer may pay us a small commission at no additional cost to you. This is disclosed at the top of any article that contains such links, in line with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's guidance on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255).

Current status

As of 2026-08-08, K-Regen Lab is not yet enrolled in any affiliate program and carries no active affiliate links. We are publishing this policy in advance so that it is in place before the first link goes live. When that changes, this page will be updated with the date and the programs involved.

How this affects what we write

  • Products are selected for coverage because of what their ingredient lists and prices show, not because of commission rates.
  • A product is never called better because it pays more. Where we name a pick, the reason is stated in the article and is traceable to disclosed data.
  • Products with no affiliate program at all are covered on the same terms as those with one, including when they compare favourably.
  • We do not disguise affiliate links, and we do not use link shorteners that hide the destination.

What we cannot promise

We do not control retailer pricing, stock, formulation changes, or shipping. Prices and availability quoted in articles reflect the date shown and can change without notice.
