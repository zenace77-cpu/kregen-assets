K-Regen Lab is an independent publication about Korean regenerative skincare - mainly PDRN - written for readers in the United States.

The category has a specific problem: brands advertise big numbers, the numbers are not comparable, and almost nobody converts them into something a buyer can act on. A "100,000ppm" claim and a "1,000ppm" claim can sit on the same shelf at nearly the same price per millilitre. We exist to do that conversion in public.

What we actually do

  • We read the ingredient lists and prices that retailers publish, and record the date we checked them.
  • We convert claims into comparable units: ppm, millilitres, price per millilitre, and cost per milligram of PDRN.
  • We separate what a brand advertises from what we can responsibly quote. When a headline number describes a blended complex, an exosome count, or something the brand never clarifies, we say so and do not present it as a PDRN concentration.
  • We name the gaps. "The brand does not disclose the concentration" is a useful sentence, and we use it often.

What we do not do

  • We do not test products. There is no lab, no trial, no before-and-after. Nothing on this site is a first-hand account of using a product, and any article that reads like one is an error we want to hear about.
  • We do not accept products, payment, or gifts in exchange for coverage or for a particular conclusion.
  • We do not give medical advice. These are cosmetics, not treatments for any condition.

How articles are produced

Research and drafting are assisted by AI language models. Every article is then checked against our own product dataset and passed through an automated editorial and compliance review before it is published. That review looks for unsupported claims, medical-sounding language, fabricated experience, and missing disclosures.

To be plain about the limits of that process: no human subject-matter expert reviews every article. We would rather say so than imply an approval that did not happen. Where an article covers a health-adjacent question, we keep the language conservative and point readers to a qualified clinician.

Known limitations

  • Prices move with sales. Every price we publish carries the date it was checked, and it may already be stale when you read it.
  • Ingredient-list position tells you the order of a list. It does not tell you a concentration, and we do not treat it as one.
  • Our dataset is built from retailer listings. Retailer copy is not independent scientific evidence, and we do not present it as such.
