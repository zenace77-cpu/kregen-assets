K-Regen Lab compares published product disclosures and prices while stating clearly what the available evidence cannot establish.

Scope of this policy

K-Regen Lab is an independent site that analyzes Korean PDRN skincare in English for readers in the United States. The site currently has 15 published articles covering approximately 20 PDRN products.

This policy explains how product facts are collected, compared, written and corrected. It also defines the limits of the site's work. For a detailed description of the comparison process, see the review methodology.

What the site examines

K-Regen Lab reads retailer-listed ingredient panels, figures published by brands and current prices. These disclosures are converted into comparable units when the available information permits it.

  • Concentration claims may be expressed in parts per million or percentages.
  • Product quantities are compared in milliliters.
  • Prices may be converted into price per milliliter.
  • When the active concentration is disclosed clearly enough, the site may calculate cost per milligram of active.

Every reported figure is published with the date on which it was checked. Prices, ingredient lists and product claims can change. A dated figure describes what the cited source showed at that time, not what it will show permanently.

Source priority

Sources are prioritized in the following order:

  1. Manufacturer product page
  2. Manufacturer ingredient disclosure
  3. Official US retailer listing
  4. Major authorized retailer
  5. Reliable secondary source

A lower-priority source may be used when a higher-priority source does not publish the required information. Conflicting disclosures are not silently combined. When sources disagree, the article identifies the conflict or states that the claim cannot be independently verified from the available disclosure.

Rules applied to product claims

No invented product facts

K-Regen Lab does not publish a price, product size, ppm figure, percentage or ingredient line without a checked source and a check date. Missing information remains missing. It is not filled with an estimate.

No conversion of proprietary complexes

A percentage assigned to a proprietary complex is not treated as the concentration of one ingredient inside that complex. For example, a disclosure for a botanical or peptide complex does not establish how much PDRN the finished product contains unless the source separately states that amount.

No concentration inferred from ingredient order

Ingredient-list position may provide limited context, but it does not reveal a precise concentration. K-Regen Lab does not calculate or imply a ppm or percentage figure from where an ingredient appears in a list.

No accusations that exceed the evidence

The site does not accuse a brand of lying. A claim that cannot be confirmed is described as one that cannot be independently verified from the available disclosure. This wording distinguishes an unresolved disclosure problem from proof of deliberate deception.

No fabricated authority or popularity signals

K-Regen Lab does not fabricate ratings, review counts, credentials, awards or citations. This rule applies both to visible article text and to structured data supplied to search engines. The site publishes no estimated reading times or review counts.

No medical equivalence

A topical cosmetic is not presented as equivalent to an injectable procedure. The site does not claim that a cosmetic treats, prevents or cures a medical condition. Readers should review the medical disclaimer for the limits of the information published here.

What the site does not do

K-Regen Lab does not test products. It operates no laboratory, conducts no clinical trial and publishes no before-and-after testing. Articles are not first-hand accounts of applying or using the products discussed.

The site does not accept products, payments or gifts in exchange for coverage or a conclusion. It does not claim that every article has been reviewed by a dermatologist, physician, cosmetic chemist or other human subject-matter expert.

How articles are produced

Research and drafting are assisted by AI language models. The resulting work is checked against the site's own product dataset and passed through an editorial and compliance review before publication.

No human subject-matter expert reviews every article. The site states this directly because an automated or internal review is not the same as independent expert approval. AI assistance does not remove the requirement to verify figures against identified sources, preserve check dates and describe uncertainty accurately.

Affiliate links

Some links may earn K-Regen Lab a commission when a reader makes a qualifying purchase. Affiliate participation is disclosed at the top of the relevant article. Affiliate links are marked with rel="sponsored nofollow noopener".

Commission does not determine which products receive coverage or what conclusion an article reaches. If a product has an unresolved disclosure problem, that problem is stated before any affiliate link to the product. Additional details appear in the affiliate disclosure.

Corrections

Readers can report an incorrect figure or source through the contact page. Reports should identify the article, disputed statement and supporting source where possible.

Confirmed corrections are made in place. A dated correction note is added so readers can see that the published record changed. Minor formatting edits that do not alter meaning may be made without a correction note.
